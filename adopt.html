How do I adopt a pet?

What are the requirements for adoption?

Adoption requirements may vary depending on the shelter or rescue organization. Generally, you will need to provide proof of identification, proof of residence, and sometimes references.

Can I adopt if I live in an apartment?

Yes, many pets can adapt well to apartment living. However, it's important to consider the pet's energy level and exercise needs.

Is there an adoption fee?

Yes, most shelters and rescue organizations charge an adoption fee. This fee helps cover the cost of caring for the animals and may include vaccinations, spaying/neutering, and microchipping.
