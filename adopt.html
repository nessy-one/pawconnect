Adoption FAQ

How do I adopt?

After finding an animal you like, visit the shelter where they're housed. The staff will guide you through the rest of the process.

How do I reserve a pet?

Click Reserve on any available card, fill in your details and preferred visit date. We'll confirm within 24 hours.

Is there an adoption fee?

None
